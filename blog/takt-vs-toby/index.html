Takt vs Toby

Toby is a bookmark board: you save tabs into collections and open them later. Takt organises the tabs you have open, automatically.

Checked 21 October 2026

Short answer:

  • Choose Toby if you want a visual library of saved links, shared with a team.
  • Choose Takt if you want your open tabs grouped automatically, with AI that runs on your computer and nothing stored on someone else's server.

Where Toby does better

  • A visual board of saved links that replaces the new-tab page.
  • Sharing collections with a team or the public, with permissions.
  • Notes, checklists and shortcuts that jump to saved pages.

FAQ

Is Toby's AI grouping on-device?

Toby doesn't say which AI provider it uses. Takt's AI grouping uses the model built into Chrome, on your computer.
