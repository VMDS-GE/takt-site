Takt vs OneTab

OneTab and Takt solve too many tabs in opposite ways. OneTab closes them into a list. Takt keeps them open and files each one into the right Chrome tab group.

Short answer:

  • Choose OneTab if you'd rather close everything into a list and come back to it later than keep tabs open.
  • Choose Takt if you keep tabs open and want them organised into Chrome tab groups as you browse, with duplicates stopped and idle tabs unloaded.

FAQ

Can I use OneTab and Takt together?

Yes. They don't overlap much: Takt organises the tabs you keep open, and OneTab archives the ones you close.

Is OneTab free?

Yes. Only its optional Backup & Sync is paid, at $39.99 per year.
