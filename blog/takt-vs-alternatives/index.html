Takt vs the alternatives: Chrome tab managers compared

There are many ways to deal with too many tabs: group them, close them into a list, save them as sessions, or move them into workspaces. Here is how Takt compares with the tools people use most, including where they do better.

Checked 21 October 2026

Short answer:

  • Takt files tabs into Chrome tab groups as they open: by your URL rules, by site category, or with AI that runs on your computer.
  • OneTab, Session Buddy and Toby save tabs for later. Workona puts them in cloud workspaces. Tab Manager Plus organises windows.
  • Ato also groups with AI, but in the cloud, and needs an account.

FAQ

Which Chrome tab managers group tabs automatically?

Of the products compared here, Takt, Tab Groups Extension and Auto-Group Tabs group tabs into Chrome tab groups by rule. Takt also groups by category or with on-device AI. Ato groups with a cloud AI.

Does Chrome group tabs automatically?

No. Chrome's tab groups are made by hand, and its AI "Tab organizer" has been retired.

Which tab manager groups tabs with AI without sending them to a server?

Takt. It uses the AI model built into Chrome, on your computer. Ato sends tab URLs and titles to Google Gemini; Toby doesn't name its AI provider.
