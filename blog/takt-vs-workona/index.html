Takt vs Workona

Workona is a workspace app: it organises tabs into its own "spaces" stored in the cloud, behind an account. Takt works with Chrome's own tab groups, needs no account and keeps your tabs in your browser.

Checked 21 October 2026

Short answer:

  • Choose Workona if you work in projects with a team and want tabs, documents and tasks in shared workspaces.
  • Choose Takt if you want Chrome's own tab groups filled automatically, without an account and without your tabs leaving your browser.

FAQ

Does Workona use Chrome tab groups?

No. Its own Tab Groups Helper listing says the Workona Tab Manager doesn't currently support Chrome tab groups.
