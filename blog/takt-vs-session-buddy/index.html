Takt vs Session Buddy

Session Buddy is a session saver: it remembers your windows and brings them back. Takt organises the tabs you have open right now.

Checked 21 October 2026

Short answer:

  • Choose Session Buddy if what you need most is to save, recover and export sessions.
  • Choose Takt if you want your open tabs organised automatically, with duplicates stopped and idle tabs unloaded. Takt Premium also saves sessions.

Where Session Buddy does better

FAQ

Does Takt save sessions like Session Buddy?

Takt Premium saves up to 50 sessions and saves a window automatically when it closes. Session Buddy goes further on history and export formats, and it's free.
