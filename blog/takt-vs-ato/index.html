Takt vs Ato: AI tab grouping, on your computer or in the cloud

Both group tabs with AI. The difference is where it runs. Ato sends each tab's URL and title to Google Gemini through its servers, behind an account. Takt uses the AI model built into Chrome, on your computer, with no account.

Short answer:

  • Choose Ato if you want grouping with no setup, and you're fine with your tab URLs and titles going to a cloud AI.
  • Choose Takt if you want AI grouping that stays on your computer, no account, and URL rules for the sites you use most.

FAQ

Does Ato send my tabs to the cloud?

Yes. Its extension sends each tab's URL and title to its server for AI grouping, and its privacy policy names Google Gemini. The policy says they are processed temporarily and not stored.

Does Takt's AI need the internet?

No. Takt uses Chrome's built-in model (the Prompt API), which runs on your computer once Chrome has downloaded it. It needs Chrome 127 or later and hardware that Chrome supports for on-device AI.
