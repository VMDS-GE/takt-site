Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Summary #

The Takt extension sends no analytics, telemetry or usage data. Your rules, settings and tabs stay in your browser's storage unless you turn on Google Drive sync, which copies your Takt data to your own Google Drive. The extension goes online only to check your license and, if you turned it on, to sync with Google Drive. When you uninstall Takt, Chrome opens an optional feedback page; what that page receives is described under Uninstall feedback.

What data Takt stores #

All data is stored locally using Chrome's built-in storage APIs (chrome.storage.sync, chrome.storage.local, chrome.storage.session):

DataStoragePurpose
Tab grouping rulessyncYour rules for organizing tabs
Extension settingssyncPreferences (theme, automation mode, etc.)
ProfilessyncNamed rule sets for different workflows
Rule match statisticslocalCounts of how many tabs each rule matched
Dismissed suggestionslocalDomains you dismissed from suggestions
License cachelocalCached payment status to avoid repeated checks
Group registrysessionMaps group names to Chrome tab group IDs (cleared on browser close)
Resource snapshotssessionTab memory/network data for the resource dashboard (cleared on browser close)

chrome.storage.sync data may be synced across your Chrome instances via your Google account if Chrome Sync is enabled. This is a Chrome platform feature, not controlled by Takt.

Interaction tracking #

The staleness reporter content script listens for click, scroll, and keydown events on each active tab. When any of those events occurs, the script records a single Unix-millisecond timestamp (Date.now()) and transmits it to the extension's service worker at most once every 30 seconds — and only if a new interaction has occurred since the last report. Tabs with no user interaction generate zero reports.

The transmitted payload contains exactly one data field: the integer timestamp. No other information is included. Specifically, the staleness reporter does not read page text, form values, keystroke characters, the tab URL, the page title, or any DOM node content. The keydown listener only records that a key was pressed, not which key.

This data stays within the extension: it is used solely to determine whether a tab is stale and is never sent to any external server.

What data Takt does NOT collect #

  • Browsing history or URLs you visit
  • Page content or form data
  • Personal information (name, email, etc.). If you connect Drive sync, your Google account email is read only to show which account is connected. The only email address Takt receives is one you choose to type into a form on this website (the newsletter or the uninstall survey).
  • Analytics or telemetry of any kind from the installed extension. The optional survey shown after you uninstall is described under Uninstall feedback.
  • Cookies or authentication tokens

Network communication #

The installed extension makes only these network requests:

  • License validation via ExtensionPay — checks whether you have an active paid subscription. This happens on extension startup and when you open the upgrade page. ExtensionPay receives a pseudonymous extension user ID (generated by Chrome), not your browsing data or personal information.
  • Google Drive sync (Premium, off by default) — only if you turn it on. Takt uses Google's API to read and write its own data files in your Drive's hidden app folder, and reads your Google account email only to show which account is connected. See Drive sync.

The extension sends nothing else. When you uninstall it, Chrome opens a feedback page; see Uninstall feedback.

Uninstall feedback #

When you uninstall Takt, Chrome (not the extension) opens a short, optional feedback page at https://taktabs.com/uninstall/. Takt sets this address in advance while it is installed. The address carries only these coarse, anonymous values:

ParameterMeaningPossible values
vExtension versionThe exact version number, such as 4.2.0
planYour planfree, premium
daysDays since install, as a range0, 1, 2-7, 8-30, 31-90, 91+
modeGrouping moderules, domain, ai, none
rulesNumber of rules, as a range0, 1-5, 6-20, 21+
langLanguageen, fr, es, it, de, pt

A value Takt cannot determine is left out. The address never includes browsing data, tab URLs, page titles, rule names or contents, your email address, your license key, or any identifier.

The survey is optional. Nothing is sent unless you click submit. If you do, the page sends to Formspark (their privacy policy), our form provider: the reason you chose, any text you typed into the answer boxes, your email address if you chose to give one, the values above, the language the page was shown in, and an anti-spam token. Like any web request, this also gives Formspark your IP address.

When the page loads, even if you never submit, two other services are contacted:

  • Botpoison (their privacy policy) protects the form from spam. It connects to api.botpoison.com, which receives your IP address, and stores a temporary session entry in the tab's sessionStorage, which is cleared when you close the tab.
  • unpkg.com, a public content delivery network, serves the Botpoison script. Like any website you load, it receives your IP address.

Why: to understand why people uninstall Takt, so we can improve it.

How long: survey submissions are deleted after 12 months.

Deletion: to have a submission deleted sooner, email takt.extension@gmail.com with the email address you entered or the approximate date you submitted.

Third-party services #

ExtensionPay (their privacy policy) processes payments for the Premium plan. Takt does not handle payment information directly. ExtensionPay uses Stripe for payment processing.

Formspark (their privacy policy) processes forms submitted on this website: email addresses submitted through the newsletter form, and uninstall survey answers (see Uninstall feedback). If you submit your email address to the newsletter, it is collected for the purpose of sending occasional product updates. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email we send.

Botpoison (their privacy policy) provides spam protection for the uninstall survey page.

unpkg.com serves the Botpoison script to the uninstall survey page.

Data deletion #

Uninstalling Takt from Chrome automatically deletes all locally stored data. To delete synced data, disable Chrome Sync or clear extension data from your Google account settings before uninstalling.

To delete an uninstall survey submission, email takt.extension@gmail.com (see Uninstall feedback).

Children's privacy #

Takt is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy #

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes will be noted in the extension's changelog.
